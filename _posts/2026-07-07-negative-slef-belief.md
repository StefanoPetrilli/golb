Still, there males for whom receiving a kick in the balls is pleasurable. And you are probably under estimating how many they are. The subreddit /r/ballbusting has x follower. Even assuming that half of them are female, there are still y thousands people that like it[^1].

Introduction: The Absurdist Hook

Start with your extreme dating metaphor. Point out that if people with highly unconventional or taboo desires can easily find enthusiastic partners, the dating market is statistically vast enough for any average person. Introduce the core premise: romantic failure is rarely a supply-and-demand issue; it is a perception issue caused by limiting beliefs.
The Financial Parallel: Perceived Poverty

Bridge to the concept of skewed perception using economics. Cite how a massive portion of the population claims they cannot afford a basic $400 emergency, yet the average consumer spends thousands annually on impulse buys. Connect this back to dating: just as people are blind to their financial leaks, they are blind to their romantic opportunities because their perception is flawed.
The Health Parallel: Genetic Fatalism

Deepen the argument by showing how negative beliefs actively cause failure. Introduce the concept of genetic fatalism from the Stanford treadmill study. Explain that when people believe they are biologically disadvantaged, their bodies and minds literally give up, creating a self-fulfilling prophecy. Compare this to the "I am inherently unlovable" victim identity.
The Neuroscience of Self-Sabotage

Tie all three examples together using neuroscience. Explain how negative internal dialogue and self-blame actively impair executive functioning and create cognitive filtering. This explains why the broke person can't see their spending, the dieter gives up, and the single person can't see the dating pool: their brain is literally filtering out the real-world evidence to protect their negative self-belief.
Conclusion: Rewiring the Internal Dialogue

End with a provocative call to action. Challenge the reader to stop blaming external factors like the dating market, the economy, or genetics, and start confronting their internal critic. Empower them to recognize that the limitations keeping them stuck are entirely in their mindset, not their reality.
